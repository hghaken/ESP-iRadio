## Layout (Rev B draft, 2026-10-15)

Board 80 x 60 mm, 4 layers (F.Cu signals and GND pour, In1.Cu solid GND plane, In2.Cu signals and
power, B.Cu signals and GND pour), 1.6 mm, every part on the top side. Rules: 0.2 mm tracks with
0.15 mm clearance, Power class 0.5 mm (VSYS, VIN, VBUS, VBAT, BATN, cell +, +3V3, GND), Supply
class 0.3 mm (+3V3A, backlight, speaker outputs), vias 0.55/0.3 mm (0.4/0.2 only where a pour
island had no room), copper >= 0.6 mm from the edge (keep-out ring). Renders in `renders/`.

Floor plan (front view, origin top left; `tools/placement.py`):
- display panel 37.43 x 30.07 mm over x 7-44.4, y 4-34.1 (outline on User.Drawings), on ~5 mm
  spacers; its FPC folds down onto J3 (x 47, pads vertical); only low parts under the panel;
- WROVER-IE horizontal under the panel, its U.FL end at the left edge outside the panel; 3.3 V
  regulator and its capacitors below the module's 3V3 pin;
- encoder SW1 top right (shaft at x 62, y 15), microSD J4 at the right edge (card from the
  right), RESET/BOOT buttons at the left edge, recovery pads TP203-TP207 below the panel;
- bottom edge from left: jack J5 (left edge), DAC U1 and LP5907, USB-C J201, charge LEDs, slide
  switch SW203, cell J202, speaker J6; CH340C and auto-reset above the USB-C; charger,
  protection and battery divider bottom right; amplifier U2 next to J6.

Routing: Freerouting 1.9.0 (DSN/SES), In1.Cu marked as a power layer so the router keeps
signals off the GND plane; ~380 GND stitching vias; SMD pads join the outer pours solid, THT pads
with thermal reliefs. The USB pair is routed like any other signal (short, but not
impedance-controlled).

Still to do on the layout (before ordering):

- J3: pad orientation and pin 1 from the paper mock-up with the real panel; R22 value;
- SW1: the short-shaft EC11 for the recessed knob (D16) and its footprint;
- SW203: which slide position is ON (silk says "ON/OFF ?");
- charge-LED labels "CHG"/"PWR" sit inside the USB-C outline (move when the LEDs move);
- silkscreen warnings come from the footprints' own outlines (JLCPCB clips silk on pads);
- two narrow necks (< 0.15 mm) in the B.Cu GND pour (warnings);
- 3D models: this PC's KiCad has no `Connector_USB` and `Rotary_Encoder` 3D folders (install
  them or use the LCSC models) before exporting the STEP for the case; the WROVER-IE model is the

## Schematic (Rev B, integrated board)

Sheet 1 `esp-iradio.kicad_sch` ("radio"): U3 ESP32-WROVER-IE-N16R8 in the middle (symbol laid out
like the former Olimex headers, so the Rev A wiring stayed), encoder left, display panel J3 (12-pin
FPC pads) + backlight driver Q1/R22/R23 and microSD J4 right, PCM5102A + jack J5 and MAX98357A
(on `VSYS`) at the bottom, sheet symbol top left. Sheet 2 `power_usb.kicad_sch`: USB-C J201 with
CC resistors, TVS, polyfuse and USBLC6-2SC6; CH340C U203 with auto-reset Q201/Q202 and the RESET /
BOOT buttons; BQ24074 U201 with its programming resistors, NTC, charge LEDs and BAT54WS isolation;
the cell: reverse P-FET Q203, JST-PH J202, DW01A U204 + FS8205A U205 in the negative lead; AP7361C
U206 (EN = slide switch SW203) and LP5907 U207 (`+3V3A`); recovery pads TP203-TP207 (EN, IO0,
TXD0, RXD0, GND) bottom left. Sheet 2 references are numbered 2xx.

Between the sheets: global power nets `VSYS`, `+3V3`, `+3V3A`, `GND` and global labels `ESP_EN`,
`ESP_IO0`, `TXD0`, `RXD0`, `CHG`, `PGOOD`, `VBUS_SENSE`, `VBAT_SENSE`. Local labels on sheet 2:
`VBUS` (connector side of the fuse, only J201 and F201), `VIN` (after F201: TVS, USBLC6 VBUS,
USB divider, BQ24074 IN), `USB_D+`/`USB_D-` (connector
side; the USBLC6 passes each line through inside, pins 1-6
and 3-4, so the CH340C side is a separate net), `VBAT`, `BATN` (cell minus), `CHG_N`, `PGOOD_N`,
`EN_3V3`.

Values set from the BQ24074 datasheet (SLUS810N): ISET 1.1k (0.81 A charge), ILIM 1.13k (1.37 A
input; the valid range starts at 1.1k, so not 1.1k at 1 %), TMR 68k (~9 h), ITERM
open (default 10 % termination; the BQ24074 has ITERM on pin 15, **no SYSOFF pin**), CE and EN1
to GND, EN2 to OUT, TS to a 10k NTC. Caps: IN 4.7 uF 25 V, OUT and BAT 10 uF; VSYS carries
~31 uF in total (datasheet 4.7-47 uF), no bulk electrolytic. ILIM mode has no VIN-DPM: power the
board from a source of at least 1.5 A (owner's choice, 07). Input: VBUS -> F201 (2 A hold) ->
D201 SMAJ5.0A -> IN; USB sense 330k/220k + 100 nF (high-ohmic for an unpowered ESP32).

Symbol libraries (project tables `sym-lib-table` / `fp-lib-table`, files in `lib/`):
- `Elektuur` (`lib/Elektuur.kicad_sym`): Elektuur-style symbols, CC0, by M. Gyger,
  https://gitlab.com/mgyger/kicad-addons v0.7.0. The library's old `Spice_*` properties were
  removed on 2026-10-15: KiCad 9 converts them to `Sim.*` fields when it loads the library, so
  every placed diode, LED, fuse and push button reported `lib_symbol_mismatch` (that, not the body
  styles, was the cause in Rev A).
- `esp-iradio` (`lib/esp-iradio.kicad_sym`, `lib/esp-iradio.pretty`): project symbols
  `ESP32-WROVER-IE_iRadio`, `Micro_SD_TF01A`, `AudioJack3_PJ320D` (TF-01A / PJ-320D pad numbers),
  `T-BJT-NPN_SOT23` and `T-IGFET-P_SOT23` (Elektuur transistors with SOT-23 pad numbers),
  `CH340C_3V3` (KiCad's CH340C with V3 passive: it is tied to VCC on 3.3 V, the power-output type
  gave a false ERC error); footprints `FPC_12P_P0.70mm_Solder` (panel FPC pads) and `SW_TS-1187A`
  (only two diagonal pads carry a net: a diagonal pair is across the switch whichever way the
  pins are paired inside). The Olimex module symbol/footprint of Rev A are still in the library.
- `lcsc` (`lib/lcsc.kicad_sym`, `lib/lcsc.pretty`, `lib/lcsc.3dshapes`): parts from the LCSC/EasyEDA
  library, made with easyeda2kicad 1.0.1
  (`easyeda2kicad --lcsc_id <C...> --full --output <dir>/lcsc`), 3D paths rewritten to
  `${KIPRJMOD}/lib/lcsc.3dshapes/...`; every pin type set (passive, AP7361C as a regulator). The
  STEP files are git-ignored (26 MB): recreate them with the same command. The public export
  (`scripts/export_public.py`) leaves out the whole `lcsc.3dshapes` folder (model licence unclear). WROVER-IE (C701352,
  footprint renamed `ESP32-WROVER-IE_SMD`, pads identical to the WROVER-E), AP7361C, FS8205A,
  TF-01A, PJ-320D, TYPE-C-31-M-12, MSK12C02, EC11E, TS-1187A (model only).
- KiCad standard libraries for the other ICs, connectors, test points and holes.
`LCSC` fields are set on every IC, transistor, diode, connector, switch and special part; plain
R/C get them from JLCPCB Tools (basic parts) when the BOM is made.

## Rev A (carrier board, history) - decisions and deviations

- References: MOD1 Olimex module (was J1/J2, two socket rows), J3 display, J4 microSD, J5 line jack, J6 speaker, J7 USB-C,
  J8 spare header, U1 PCM5102APWR, U2 MAX98357AETE+T (TQFN-16; was PAM8302AASCR), U3 LM66100DCK, SW1 EC11, F1 polyfuse,
  D1 TVS, FB1/FB2 ferrites, H1-H4 M3 holes, TP1-TP8 test points.
- Passives in 0603 (10 uF in 0805), JLCPCB basic-part sizes.
- Power: TVS **SMAJ5.0CA** (bidirectional, KiCad `Device:D_TVS`) on `VBUS_C`, polyfuse 2 A hold to
  `+5V_USBC`, 10 uF; LM66100 `+5V_USBC` -> `+5V_OLI` (CE to GND, ST open) with 1 uF out; ferrite FB2
  (600 R @ 100 MHz, 2 A) from `+5V_USBC` to `+VAMP` (keeps amplifier switching noise off the
  radio's 5 V).
- Display/SD: series resistors at the ESP32 side of SCK/MOSI: R8 22 Ohm on SCK (softer clock
  edges, less interference next to the WROVER antenna; owner's choice 2026-10-12), R9 0 Ohm on
  MOSI (placeholder, 22-33 Ohm if ringing shows); bus side nets `SPI_SCK_BUS`,
  `SPI_MOSI_BUS`; pull-ups 10k on LCD_CS, SD_CS, MISO, DAT1, DAT2.
- DAC (TI PCM510xA datasheet SLAS859C, Figure 33): 0.1 uF + 10 uF on DVDD, CPVDD, AVDD; 2.2 uF
  flying (CAPP-CAPM) and on VNEG; 0.1 uF on LDOO; 470 Ohm + 2.2 nF per output; AVDD through
  ferrite FB1 as `+3V3A`; SCK/FMT/FLT/DEMP to GND, XSMT 10k to 3V3.
- Amplifier (2026-10-15, owner's request after the breadboard test): **MAX98357AETE+T** instead of
  the PAM8302A. It takes I2S directly (DIN/BCLK/LRCLK on the same nets as the PCM5102A), so the
  analog mono sum (R18/R19 180k, C20, R20 91k, C21) is gone. SD_MODE on `AMP_SD` (GPIO2) with
  R21 1M to `+VAMP` (was a 100k pull-down): released = ~0.45 V = on, (L+R)/2; GPIO2 low = off.
  GAIN_SLOT open (9 dB, as on the tested breakout). Supply 10 uF (C22) + 100 nF (C23, was 1 uF)
  + 220 uF (C24). ERC: one warning left, the exposed pad (pin type unspecified) on the GND net
  with power-input pins; harmless.
- PWR_FLAGs on `+5V_USBC`, `+VAMP`, `GND`, `+3V3`, `+3V3A` (sources come through passive pins).

## Tooling pitfall

The KiCad MCP server (v2.8.2) created new sheets in the **KiCad 10 format** (`version 20260101`,
`(body_style ...)`, `(in_pos_files ...)` in every placed symbol). KiCad 9.0.9 then silently skips
those sheets: ERC "0 violations" and an empty netlist. Fixed by rewriting the headers to
`20250114` / `"9.0"` and removing those two tokens (the server only writes them into files that
declare KiCad 10), and by patching the server's `python/templates/*.kicad_sch` headers to KiCad 9
(redo after updating the server). Always check `kicad-cli sch export netlist` for a non-empty
component list, not only the ERC count.
Do not run the server's `lint_schematic_cosmetic` pass `orient_labels` on these sheets: on
2026-10-11 it turned 78 labels of the audio sheet to point into their own symbols (text over the
capacitor and resistor bodies). Fixed with a script that points every pin label away from its
symbol (KiCad 9: angle 0 justify left, 90 left, 180 right, 270 right).
